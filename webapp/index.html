The idea, as a coffee shop

You want to predict customers every hour for the next 4 days, using the last 30 days of hourly records.

1

Remove the average

"We get 50 customers an hour on average." Keep only the ups and downs.

x − mean(x)
2

Smooth the noise

Blend each hour with its neighbours so one tour bus doesn't mislead you.

conv1d(x) + x
3

Line up the same hour

Make 24 lists: "every 8 am", "every 9 am", … each holding 30 days.

720 → 24 × 30
4

One shared rule

Learn one rule for how a list moves from day to day, and use it for all 24 hours.

Linear 30 → 4
5

Put it back together

Interleave the 24 forecasts back into hourly order and add the average back.

24 × 4 → 96
